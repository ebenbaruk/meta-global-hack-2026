# Brand assets

Illustrations are 1024×1024 WebP with transparency. Photos are opaque WebP.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `mascot-wave.webp` | Ford, the paper-card mascot, waving: landing hero and onboarding greeting. |
| `mascot-coffee.webp` | Ford with a coffee cup and a thumbs up: success and "morning brief ready" moments. |
| `mascot-sleep.webp` | Ford napping on a pillow: "all caught up" empty state. |
| `mascot-scan.webp` | Ford with a magnifying glass and envelopes: loading and "reading your inbox" state. |
| `sticker-starburst.webp` | Yellow starburst badge with an empty center for overlaid HTML text (e.g. "New", "Free"). |
| `doodle-sparkle.webp` | Sparkle burst doodle for decoration around headlines and stickers. |
| `doodle-arrow.webp` | Loopy forward arrow doodle pointing up-right, for callouts. |
| `doodle-squiggle.webp` | Wavy squiggle line for underlines and section dividers. |
| `photo-maya.webp` | Maya in the Ember & Oak roastery at dawn, checking her phone (1600×1067). |
| `photo-roastery.webp` | Hands sifting fresh beans in the roaster cooling tray (1600×1067). |
| `photo-bags.webp` | Kraft coffee bags with blank lilac, yellow and mint labels on a shelf (1200×1500). |
| `photo-counter.webp` | Café counter in morning sun, barista serving a latte with pastries (1200×1500). |
