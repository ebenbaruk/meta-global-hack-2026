# Feedforward — Stop prompting. Start scrolling.

> Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon 2026 · **Indie track** · Theme: *Build for Your World*

## The insight

> "The AI feed will be the form factor of 2027.
> Most people don't know what to prompt, and they won't prompt their way into complicated agentic workflows. With chat, we ask users for digital labor: figure out what AI can do, explain the task, provide the context, repeat. That's a lot to ask if we want billions of people using agents every day.
> Meanwhile, people already spend hours in feeds. They know how to scroll, discover something useful, and act on it.
> An intelligent system that knows you should bring you the next useful action. You approve, edit, dismiss, or jump into chat to go deeper. That's how people will discover what agents can do, without having to invent a prompt first.
> And you can put ads in a feed — one of the biggest business models on the internet."
>
> — the tweet that started it

**Meta invented the feed, and the feed pays for Meta.** Feedforward shows what the feed becomes when agents fill it: not content to consume, but **work already done, waiting for one tap**. Everyone else at this hackathon is building a chatbot. We're building the one interface three billion people already know how to use.

## The product

Feedforward is a vertical, Instagram-style feed. Each card is an action that a Muse Spark agent has already prepared:

- 📨 **The email you need to answer**, already drafted in your voice.
- 📊 **Yesterday's sales numbers**, with an update to your team ready to send.
- 📅 **A scheduling conflict**, already resolved with a proposed new slot.
- 📸 **The Instagram post for your new product**, with its visual generated by Muse Image.
- 🧾 **A photo you just took** (receipt, fridge, whiteboard) turned into cards: expense report, recipe and grocery list, meeting notes.
- ⭐ **Sponsored**, clearly labeled: a relevant service that matches your actual plans, with a transparent *"Why am I seeing this?"*

Every card has four gestures: **Approve** (the agent executes), **Edit**, **Dismiss**, and **Dive in** (a chat that already knows the full context).

**The feed learns live.** Every approve and dismiss updates your taste profile, the ranking adapts, and you can watch it happen ("Tuned: fewer newsletter cards"). It's a recommendation algorithm, but it recommends *actions*.

## Why it wins (Indie criteria: creativity · technical ambition · does it work)

1. **Creativity**: a new form factor for AI, not another chat box. The story writes itself for Meta: the feed is their DNA and their business model.
2. **Technical ambition**: a real multi-agent pipeline (Miner → Preparers → Ranker → Executor) running on **three Meta models in production** (Muse Spark, Muse Image, SAM 3.1), each with a clear role, plus a live "Agent Lens" that shows every model call under the hood.
3. **It works**: everything is generated live, and Approve actually executes the action against a sandboxed toolset. Nothing is hardcoded.

## Meta models at the core

| Building block | Meta model | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Signal Miner** | Muse Spark 1.3 (structured output) | Reads the context (inbox, calendar, sales, notes) and extracts "action opportunities" |
| **Preparers** | Muse Spark 1.3 (strict JSON schema per card kind) | Do the complete work behind each card |
| **Ranker + Taste memory** | Muse Spark 1.3 + preference weights | Ranks the feed and learns from every gesture |
| **Visuals** | Muse Image 1.0 | Card visuals, social post images, mockups |
| **Camera → cards** | Muse Spark vision + SAM 3.1 | Snap a photo, SAM segments the objects, cards appear |
| **Dive in chat** | Muse Spark / Muse Wonder | A context-loaded conversation about a card |
| **Sponsored** | Muse Spark | Ads matched to your plans, transparent and labeled |

## The demo (2–3 min video)

Persona: **Maya**, solo founder of a small specialty coffee roastery (a fictional brand). Her inbox, calendar, sales data and notes are realistic sandbox data; the cards are generated live.

1. Open the app. The feed materializes as agents work (with Agent Lens visible on the side).
2. Approve a drafted reply to a wholesale client. It's sent and appears in the outbox.
3. Dismiss two low-value cards. The feed retunes itself, visibly.
4. Approve an Instagram post. Muse Image renders the visual.
5. Snap a photo of a supplier invoice. SAM 3.1 outlines the items, and new cards appear (expense logged, reorder suggested).
6. A sponsored card appears that fits her plans, with "Why am I seeing this?".
7. Dive in: a chat that already knows everything about the card.

Closing line: *"Chat asks you for labor. The feed gives you leverage."*

## Business model

Same as the tweet, and the same as Meta's: a **sponsored action** inside a feed of useful work, where relevance comes from your plans, not from surveillance of your clicks. It's labeled, explainable, and dismissible, and dismissals train the feed too.
