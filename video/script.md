# Feedforward — demo video script (target ≈ 2:45, hard cap 3:00)

Voice: `en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural` via edge-tts. Source of truth for the narration: `scripts/video-scenes.ts`. One clip per scene, scenes cut on the clip length. Waits on live model calls are sped up in the edit and labeled with a yellow "Sped up N×" pill.

Footage: `landing` = the marketing page at `/` (1440×810 → 1080p) · `desktop` = the app inside its phone frame, with Maya's context and taste on the left and the Agent Lens on the right · `mobile` = the app full bleed on a 390×844 phone, placed on a cream stage with a caption.
