# Feedforward — demo video script (target 2:50)

Voice: `en-US-AndrewNeural` via edge-tts. One clip per scene, scenes cut on the clip length.

| # | Scene | Visual | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook (0:00) | Title slide: "Stop prompting. Start scrolling." | Every AI product today hands you an empty box and asks: what do you want? Most people don't know. |
| 2 | Problem (0:10) | Slide: chat = labor (figure out, explain, give context, repeat) | Chat asks you for labor. Figure out what the AI can do, explain the task, paste the context, repeat. But three billion people already know an interface that asks nothing of them. The feed. |
| 3 | Intro (0:30) | Phone view, feed materializing, skeletons with live labels | This is Feedforward. Meet Maya, she runs a small coffee roastery. She opens the app, and agents running on Muse Spark have already read her inbox, her calendar and her sales, and are preparing work for her, live. |
| 4 | Approve (0:50) | Email reply card → swipe right → toast → outbox | The wholesale café wants pricing before Friday. The reply is already drafted, in her voice. One swipe, and it's sent. |
| 5 | Team update (1:02) | Team update card, metrics + sparkline | Yesterday's web sales jumped by a third. The numbers are pulled, and the update for her team is ready to post. |
| 6 | Learn (1:12) | Dismiss two newsletter cards → "Tuned" toast → taste weights move | She doesn't care about newsletters. Two dismissals, and the feed retunes itself. It's a recommendation algorithm, but it recommends actions. |
| 7 | Muse Image (1:27) | Social post card, image rendering → lands | Her new coffee launches Friday. The launch post is written, and Muse Image renders the visual. |
| 8 | Snap + SAM (1:40) | Snap tab → invoice photo → SAM outlines draw → cards appear | The physical world feeds the feed too. She snaps a supplier invoice. Muse Spark reads it, SAM 3.1 segments it pixel by pixel, and the expense is ready to log. |
| 9 | Sponsored (1:58) | Sponsored card, "Why am I seeing this?" | And because it's a feed, it pays for itself. Sponsored actions match her plans this week, not her browsing. Labeled, explained, and one swipe away from gone. |
| 10 | Dive in (2:12) | Dive in → chat → "Make it shorter" → apply | When she wants to go deeper, she dives in. The chat already knows the full context. |
| 11 | Under the hood (2:24) | Desktop: Agent Lens + architecture slide | Under the hood: a Miner finds opportunities, parallel Preparers do the work, a Ranker learns from every gesture, and an Executor acts. Four Meta models, every call visible in the Agent Lens. |
| 12 | Close (2:42) | Closing slide | Chat asks you for labor. The feed gives you leverage. Feedforward. |
